Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026. Durable Set Log is designed so your workout record can stay yours.

What stays on your device

Routines, workout sessions, sets, corrections, and a purchased license token are stored in your browser profile using IndexedDB or localStorage. We do not receive this workout data. Export and backup files are created locally and go only where you choose to save or share them.

Billing and license verification

If you purchase or verify a license, the app contacts the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, processes checkout details under its own policies. Durable Set Log sends the license token necessary to verify access; it does not receive card details.

Network and analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking SDK, third-party font, or behavioral analytics. The hosting service may keep short-lived security and aggregate request logs, such as requested path, timestamp, and IP address. They are not combined with your workout ledger.

Your choices

Export CSV or JSON at any time. You can erase local data through your browser’s site-data controls; this cannot be undone unless you made a backup. Contact privacy@sociobot.in for privacy questions.
